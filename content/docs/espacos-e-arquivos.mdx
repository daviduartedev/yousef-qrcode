## Workspace roles

Each account has a personal workspace. Authorized users can manage members, invitations, and roles in [Organization](/settings/organization). Select the correct workspace before creating or editing content.

| Role | Main responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Owner | Manage the organization and its owners. |
| Admin | Manage team operations within the permissions of the role. |
| Editor | Create, edit, and publish workspace content. |
| Viewer | View permitted content and data. |

The server verifies access to each resource. Invitations expire and must be accepted by the intended account. Email delivery for invitations, verification, and password recovery depends on the configured provider.

## Asset library

Use the [Asset Library](/assets) to upload supported files, group them into folders, and pick media in supported editors. The server verifies each file's type, size, and access. File QR codes and media attached to published content follow their own publication rules.

## Account preferences

The product lets you change the language and theme on your device. Account-level preferences, help, and notifications are part of [Lot 20](/docs/status).
