## Linkpages

In [Linkpages](/linkpages), start with a template and edit the profile, images, theme, and blocks. You can add links, text, and contact actions. A draft stays private until published. Check the public link and associated QR on a phone. Publish changes to update the public version.

## Digital business cards

In [Cards](/cards), set up the card profile and design, then publish to share its link and QR code. Visitors can use the configured contact actions, save a vCard, or share their own details after consenting where contact exchange is enabled. [Contacts](/cards/contacts) lists submissions available to the workspace.

## Forms

In [Forms](/forms), select a template, configure questions, required fields, and any notice or consent text. Publish after reviewing the draft. Submit a test response through the public URL and check the responses screen and CSV export. You can stop accepting new responses while keeping existing records.

The verification of external notifications, event workflows, and AI drafting is tracked in [Delivery status](/docs/status).
