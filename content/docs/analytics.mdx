## QR codes

[Overview](/overview) shows recent activity and links to QR lifecycle filters. Open [Analytics](/analytics) to select a date range, compare it with the previous period, and filter by code, device, or country. Review totals, daily trends, rankings, device, operating system, browser, and hour. Export results as CSV.

Tracked redirects provide scan analytics. Unique scanners and deduplication depend on the available signals and trusted proxy configuration. Location data may be unavailable. Location sharing on advanced QR codes follows a separate consent flow.

## Linkpages, cards, and forms

- Linkpages track views and clicks on published content.
- Cards report views, actions, and contacts received where enabled.
- Forms show activity and responses. Exports are available only to authorized workspace members.

When checking a report, use synthetic data and verify the chosen date range, counting window, and CSV totals.
