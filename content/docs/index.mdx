## Start with your goal

- **Create and share a QR code.** Choose the content, customize the design, and download an image to print or share. [QR code guide](/docs/qr-codes)
- **Measure performance.** Review scans by QR code, period, device, and country when available. [Analytics](/docs/analytics)
- **Publish content.** Build a link page, digital business card, or form and share its address. [Linkpages, cards, and forms](/docs/paginas-e-cartoes)
- **Work with your team.** Switch workspaces, manage access, and reuse uploaded files. [Workspaces and assets](/docs/espacos-e-arquivos)
- **Connect other tools.** Use scoped API keys, signed webhooks, or a supported connector. [API and integrations](/docs/api-e-integracoes)

## Access the product

[Sign in to QRConnect](/auth). The product supports English and Arabic, with a right-to-left layout in Arabic.

Read [Architecture](/docs/arquitetura) for the technical stack and [Delivery status](/docs/status) for verified features, differences from Uniqode, and remaining work.
